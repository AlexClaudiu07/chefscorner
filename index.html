What kind of game is Chef's Corner? ▾

Chef’s Corner is a cozy cooking & management game where you run your own kitchen. Make burgers, pizza, hotdogs, smoothies, and more, serve happy customers, and upgrade your kitchen to become the most successful chef in the neighborhood!

Is this a stressful or competitive game? ▾

Not at all. Chef's Corner has zero punishment mechanics. You won't get penalized for mistakes, missed timing, or playing slowly. The whole philosophy is: cook at your pace, enjoy the vibe.

What platforms will it launch on? ▾

PC via Steam is the primary platform. Console and mobile ports may follow after launch. Wishlisting on Steam is the best way to stay updated.

When is the release date? ▾

No fixed date yet — the game will ship when it's polished and ready. Wishlist on Steam and join the Discord for regular updates.

Can I play solo or is there multiplayer? ▾

Right now, Chef’s Corner is single-player, focused on your personal cooking and management experience.
